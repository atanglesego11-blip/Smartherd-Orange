# Data ethics

## What is in this repository today

No real animal, farmer, or cattlepost appears anywhere in this build. All
telemetry is synthetic, generated by `simulator/simulate_collar.py` from
disclosed parameters. The coordinates are a fictional cattlepost.

## Rules for when real data arrives

**Location is identifying.** A cattlepost coordinate identifies a household
and, by implication, the household's wealth. Published or shared data is
aggregated to district level. Exact coordinates stay with the farmer and with
DVS where a notifiable disease requires it.

**Consent is specific and revocable.** A farmer agreeing to herd monitoring
has not agreed to have their herd's health history shared with a buyer, an
insurer, or a researcher. Each of those is a separate ask. Consent can be
withdrawn, and withdrawal deletes.

**A health alert is not a disease record.** SmartHerd.ai produces suspicions, not
diagnoses. A suspicion must never become a mark against a farmer's animals in
any market or traceability system. Only a veterinary officer's finding does
that.

**Theft alerts are sensitive in both directions.** An alert says an animal is
moving, not that a person is a thief. Under *mafisa*, the person holding an
animal is often not its owner, and a lawful transfer can look exactly like a
theft. SmartHerd.ai must never name a suspect, and the interface must not invite the
farmer to.

## Indigenous knowledge

Knowledge recorded in `data/indigenous_knowledge/` belongs to the communities
and individuals it came from. It is published under MIT so that it stays open
and cannot be enclosed by anyone, including this project's authors.

- Contributors are named unless they ask not to be.
- Nothing is marked `verified` without at least two unrelated herders from
  different areas.
- Where a term is unknown, the gap stays visible. For anthrax and rabies, an
  invented Setswana term is a safety problem, not a translation problem.
- Knowledge we cannot yet act on is recorded anyway. A system that only keeps
  the knowledge it finds convenient is not preserving anything.

## What the system says about itself

Every alert SmartHerd.ai raises carries the line that it flags behaviour and does
not diagnose, and that a veterinary officer decides. That text is in
`ml/risk_engine.py` and is not optional.
